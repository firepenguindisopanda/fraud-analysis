=== Section 1.13 - Recall-Focused Refinement ===

Lowering decision threshold increases recall at the cost of precision.

  LR    best F1 @ 0.30:  F1=0.137  Recall=0.908  Precision=0.074
  RF    best F1 @ 0.30:  F1=0.667  Recall=0.888  Precision=0.534
  XGB   best F1 @ 0.30:  F1=0.762  Recall=0.867  Precision=0.680

Key finding: Lowering threshold from 0.5 to 0.3-0.4 improves both recall and F1 for most models.
The best threshold depends on your tolerance for false positives.
